# CarefreeBag

![CarefreeBag](media/logo.png)

**Questing or in a dungeon, you never have to think about your inventory.**

CarefreeBag is a simple bag addon for WoW Forever. It does the boring bag chores for you, so you can keep playing.

## Why CarefreeBag?
- **No rules to set up.** Drag an item onto a group's empty slot. Done. It stays there from now on.
- **It does your chores.** Grey items are sold at a vendor, half stacks are combined, and your AH items and BoEs are mailed to your alts with one click.
- **Calm and simple.** One window, the classic Blizzard look, and only a few options.

## Features
- **Works out of the box**: ready-made categories (Consumables, Professions, Gear, and more in the options) fill themselves. Drag an item onto any group and it stays there, per character.
- Fixed groups: **Quest**, **BoE**, **Auto Sell**, **Other** and **Keys** (off by default). Each can be turned off.
- **Auto Sell**: grey items, plus anything you drop in, are sold when you open a vendor. Hold **Shift** to skip once.
- **Stacks**: the same item shows as one tile; half stacks are combined in your bags (never during combat).
- **Mail to alts**: give a group an address. At a mailbox you're asked per group: Yes, send or Skip.
- **Per group**: collapse it, and (optional) pick the sorting: stack price, newest last or manual (drag to reorder).
- **AH value** of a stack after the AH cut in the tooltip, if you have Auctionator.
- Search box, bag fill bar, hide items you never need (like your Hearthstone), new items flash once.
- English, Nederlands, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Português and Русский. Picks your client language automatically.

## Usage
- **B** opens and closes your bags.
- Minimap button: **left-click** bags, **right-click** options, **Shift-click** turns CarefreeBag on or off.
- `/cfb` options · `/cfb log` shows the log (handy for bug reports).

## Works with
- WoW Forever.
- Optional: **Auctionator** (for AH prices).
- If another bag addon (Baganator, Bagnon, AdiBags, ...) is installed, CarefreeBag asks which one you want to use.
